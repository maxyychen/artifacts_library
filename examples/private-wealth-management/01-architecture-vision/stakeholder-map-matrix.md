# Stakeholder Map Matrix

*Demo data for Silverline Private Wealth (fictitious company).*

## Purpose

Classifies stakeholders in the Unified Household Platform program by power
and interest, to determine the engagement approach for each.

## Matrix

| Stakeholder | Power/Influence | Interest Level | Key Concern | Engagement Approach |
|-------------|------------------|-----------------|-------------|----------------------|
| CEO | High | High | Client retention and growth | Manage Closely |
| COO | High | High | No disruption to advisor workflow or trading | Manage Closely |
| CCO | High | High | Regulatory and books-and-records exposure | Manage Closely |
| CTO | Medium | High | Technical feasibility and data integrity | Manage Closely |
| Financial Advisors | Medium | High | No increase in manual reconciliation work | Keep Informed |
| Clients | Low | High | Timely, consistent reporting across channels | Keep Informed |
| Birchwood Wealth Partners Leadership | Medium | Medium | Retention of acquired firm's client relationships | Keep Satisfied |

## Legend

Power/Influence and Interest Level use High / Medium / Low. Engagement
Approach follows the standard power-interest grid: Manage Closely, Keep
Satisfied, Keep Informed, Monitor.
