# Architecture Vision: Unified Household Platform

*Demo data for Silverline Private Wealth (fictitious company).*

## Vision Statement

By Q4 2027, Silverline Private Wealth will give every advisor and client a
single, reconciled, near-real-time view of household wealth across all
custodians and account types, replacing today's fragmented, partly
spreadsheet-dependent reporting process, while meeting SEC books-and-records
and fiduciary requirements without exception.

## Scope

- In scope: Client onboarding, financial planning, portfolio accounting and
  trading, performance reporting, advisory fee billing, compliance
  surveillance, client portal.
- Out of scope: HR, general ledger/corporate accounting, marketing/lead-gen
  systems (covered by a separate initiative).

## Business Goals & Drivers

| Driver | Description |
|--------|--------------|
| Custodial data fragmentation | Households commonly hold accounts across 2-3 custodians with no single reconciled view |
| Regulatory pressure | Recent SEC exam finding on books-and-records retention; Reg BI requires timely, demonstrable suitability documentation |
| M&A integration | 2026 acquisition of Birchwood Wealth Partners must be integrated onto one platform |
| Next-gen client expectations | Adult children of aging UHNW clients expect a self-service digital portal, not a quarterly PDF statement |
| Advisor productivity | Advisors spend an estimated 30% of their time manually reconciling data across CRM and portfolio systems instead of serving clients |

## Stakeholders & Concerns

| Stakeholder | Concern |
|-------------|---------|
| CEO | Client retention and growth through the integration |
| COO | No disruption to advisor workflow or trading operations during rollout |
| CCO | Regulatory and books-and-records compliance throughout migration |
| CTO | Technical feasibility, security, and data integrity |
| Financial Advisors | No increase in manual reconciliation work |
| Clients | Timely, accurate, and consistent reporting across every channel |

## High-Level Value Proposition

A single household view is projected to cut advisor reconciliation time by
roughly 60%, freeing up the equivalent of one day per week per advisor for
client-facing work, while a unified client portal is targeted to lift Net
Promoter Score by 20 points within 12 months of full rollout.

## Constraints

- SEC 17a-4 books-and-records (WORM) retention must be maintained without
  lapse throughout migration.
- No downtime permitted for advisor-facing systems during trading hours
  (9:30am-4:00pm ET) or month-end/quarter-end reporting windows.
- Birchwood Wealth Partners' legacy Foxglove PMS contract runs through
  mid-2027.
