# Capability Assessment: Unified Household Platform

*Demo data for Silverline Private Wealth (fictitious company).*

## Purpose

Assesses current vs. target maturity of the capabilities underpinning the
Unified Household Platform program, to prioritize investment.

## Assessment

| Capability | Current Maturity (1-5) | Target Maturity (1-5) | Gap | Priority |
|------------|--------------------------|--------------------------|-----|----------|
| Unified Household View | 1 | 4 | 3 | High |
| Real-Time Custodian Reconciliation | 2 | 4 | 2 | High |
| Automated Compliance Surveillance | 2 | 4 | 2 | High |
| Digital Client Portal | 2 | 5 | 3 | Medium |
| Goals-Based Financial Planning | 3 | 4 | 1 | Medium |
| Advisory Fee Billing Accuracy | 3 | 5 | 2 | Medium |

## Maturity Scale

| Level | Description |
|-------|--------------|
| 1 | Ad hoc / manual |
| 2 | Repeatable at unit level, inconsistent across org |
| 3 | Defined and standardized |
| 4 | Managed with metrics |
| 5 | Optimizing continuously |

## Notes

- The three "High" priority capabilities map directly to gaps G-02, G-03,
  and G-04 in [[06-opportunities-and-solutions/gap-analysis]].
- Low current maturity on Unified Household View is driven almost entirely
  by the unintegrated Birchwood Wealth Partners acquisition (Foxglove PMS).
