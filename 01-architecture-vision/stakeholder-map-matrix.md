# Stakeholder Map Matrix

*Demo data for Meridian Retail Group (fictitious company).*

## Purpose

Classifies stakeholders in the Unified Commerce Platform program by power and
interest, to determine the engagement approach for each.

## Matrix

| Stakeholder | Power/Influence | Interest Level | Key Concern | Engagement Approach |
|-------------|------------------|-----------------|-------------|----------------------|
| COO | High | High | No disruption to store operations | Manage Closely |
| CMO | High | High | Unified customer profile enabling personalization | Manage Closely |
| CFO | High | Medium | Program cost and payback period | Keep Satisfied |
| CISO | Medium | High | Data breach exposure from consolidation | Manage Closely |
| Store Associates | Low | High | POS speed and ease of use | Keep Informed |
| Customer Service | Low | Medium | Lack of direct service ownership (see G-01) | Keep Informed |
| Coastal Living Leadership | Medium | Medium | Retention of acquired brand's operating model | Keep Satisfied |

## Legend

Power/Influence and Interest Level use High / Medium / Low. Engagement
Approach follows the standard power-interest grid: Manage Closely, Keep
Satisfied, Keep Informed, Monitor.
