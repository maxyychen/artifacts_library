# Capability Assessment: Commerce Capabilities

*Demo data for Meridian Retail Group (fictitious company).*

## Purpose

Assesses current vs. target maturity of the business capabilities underpinning
the Unified Commerce Platform program, to prioritize investment.

## Assessment

| Capability | Current Maturity (1-5) | Target Maturity (1-5) | Gap | Priority |
|------------|--------------------------|--------------------------|-----|----------|
| Omnichannel Order Management | 2 | 4 | 2 | High |
| Unified Customer Data | 1 | 4 | 3 | High |
| Real-Time Inventory Visibility | 2 | 5 | 3 | High |
| Automated Disaster Recovery | 2 | 4 | 2 | Medium |
| Loyalty & Personalization | 3 | 4 | 1 | Medium |
| Store Associate Enablement | 3 | 3 | 0 | Low |

## Maturity Scale

| Level | Description |
|-------|--------------|
| 1 | Ad hoc / manual |
| 2 | Repeatable at unit level, inconsistent across org |
| 3 | Defined and standardized |
| 4 | Managed with metrics |
| 5 | Optimizing continuously |

## Notes

- The three "High" priority capabilities map directly to gaps G-02, G-03, and
  G-04 in [[06-opportunities-and-solutions/gap-analysis]].
